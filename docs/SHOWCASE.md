# Showcase context

## A useful decision

A supplier extract can look complete in a spreadsheet while containing missing records, old timestamps, or successful downloads of unusable data. A delivery or transformation lead needs a short answer: what can proceed, what needs correction, and what remains unobserved?

This example records explicit expectations and produces inspectable evidence from a small local input. The broken scenario makes several independent failure modes visible. The empty scenario makes the absence of evidence visible.

## Competency the repository can demonstrate

- Translate business expectations into a bounded, configurable data contract.
- Distinguish data freshness, completeness, acquisition success, and rule compliance.
- Provide the same evidence to a person, a script, and an agent without requiring a model.
- Reduce adoption effort through synthetic examples, documented denominators, and local execution.
- Keep a small component independent of a larger product.

The source illustrates these practices. It does not substantiate enterprise deployment history, customer outcomes, proprietary technology, or a claim that a capable coding model could not reproduce it.

## Sensible extensions

A consuming organization could schedule this CLI, map its source columns, add a business-specific rule, or store reports under its own retention policy. Each addition should have an actual use case. Production integrations, access control, observability, and representative evaluations require separate work.

## Scope discipline

Only code written for this standalone demonstration and synthetic fixtures belong here. Private platform code, internal prompts, infrastructure details, and customer data are outside the showcase.
